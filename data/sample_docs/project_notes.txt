Project: Chief of Staff AI agent

Goal: build a personal AI assistant that combines RAG over personal documents,
a multi-agent orchestrator, voice input/output, and a multi-layer memory system
(working, episodic, semantic, procedural).

Current phase: Phase 1 — text-only orchestrator and RAG. Once this is stable,
move to Phase 2 (tool-calling agents), then Phase 3 (memory layers), then
Phase 4 (voice), then Phase 5 (proactive check-ins).

Target audience for the portfolio version: hiring managers evaluating for
senior AI/agentic engineering roles. The project needs an eval suite, cost
and latency tracking, and clear documentation of design trade-offs to be
credible at that level — not just a working demo.
